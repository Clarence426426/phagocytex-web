Phagocytex recognized in PolyU-MicroFund Cohort 1.

Phagocytex was named an awarded team in PolyU-MicroFund 2025-26 Cohort 1, supporting early-stage translational development and venture creation around the pCAR-M platform.

The recognition supports the team's work to advance peptide-linked CAR-Macrophage technology from academic invention toward a company platform for solid tumor therapy.
